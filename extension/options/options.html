How to capture

  1. On any website, press Alt+R or click the Riff icon.
  2. Click the element you like. ↑ picks the bigger container, ↓ goes back, Esc cancels.
  3. Hold your Wispr key and say why you like it, then press Ctrl+Enter.

Change the shortcut at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.
